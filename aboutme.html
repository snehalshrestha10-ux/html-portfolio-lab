ABout me

I am Snehal Shrestha, a curious and ambitious student from Nepal with a passion for learning, problem-solving, and technology. After achieving a GPA of 4.0 in the SEE examination, I joined St. Xavier's College, Maitighar, to pursue my higher secondary education. I enjoy exploring subjects like physics, chemistry, mathematics, and computer science because they challenge me to think logically and creatively. I am also interested in programming, web development, and building projects that help me develop practical skills. I believe in understanding concepts deeply instead of relying on memorization, and I always try to improve myself through continuous learning. While I can be a bit reserved in social situations, I value meaningful connections, discipline, and personal growth. My goal is to keep learning, embrace new challenges, and use my knowledge to make a positive difference in the future.
